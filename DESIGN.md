# Texas Grid Watch: design direction

Reading this as: public energy-data explainer for curious non-experts, editorial data-journalism language, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- **Palette**: warm paper neutral background, near-black ink, one accent (clay red, used only for links, active controls and focus). Fuel colors are a data encoding, identical on every chart and in both themes.
- **Type**: Newsreader (serif) for headlines and chart titles, so pages read like articles; IBM Plex Sans for UI, labels and numbers, which has clear tabular figures.
- **Identity motif**: a thin ink rule above every chart title, borrowed from newspaper graphics.
- **Motion**: hover and focus states only, so the charts carry the attention.
- **Themes**: light and dark are both first-class.
